# Kindle ScummVM Game Compatibility (WIP)
`ctrl/cmd + f` to find your game. This is an exact match of the [ScummVM Wiki](https://wiki.scummvm.org/index.php?title=Category:Supported_Games) with this projects compatibility.

Testing is highly welcomed

### Key:
⭐ - Fully Playable with no issues
🟩 - Playable but minor issues(e.g. slow FPS, no Audio, small graphical errors, etc)
🟨 - Playable but has major issues(e.g. audio skips, screen flashing, really slow FPS, etc)
🟥 - Not Supported or UnPlayable
